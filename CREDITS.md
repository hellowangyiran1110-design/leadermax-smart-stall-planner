# Maps, routing and example data

The Birmingham map is rendered with MapLibre GL JS. OpenFreeMap supplies the map tiles, which use data from © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Postcodes.io converts the starting UK postcode into coordinates. When the trader requests a walking route, Valhalla estimates the route, distance and walking time. The market comparison uses straight-line distance from the starting point.

Stall fees, trading time slots and footfall are examples created for the prototype. The stall dashboard uses simulated orders to show how revenue, units sold and remaining stock change during a trading session.

## Crystal collection artwork

The nine crystal garden illustrations and the cabinet background were generated with ChatGPT. The collection page displays each crystal garden as a separate cutout against the cabinet background. As visitors move through the collection, the page shows the selected product’s name, description and simulated sales count.
